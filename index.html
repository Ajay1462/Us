Chapter 01 · The first frame

We met at the gym.

It started with conversations about life, our professional plans, school, and where we were going. Nothing dramatic. Just two people getting to know each other.

Then we went on a date to a temple. And somehow, that simple beginning became the first page of something that mattered a lot to me.

Before we knew the story.We were just two people at the beginning of it.
Anime interlude · 01

Some stories begin quietly.

A gym. A conversation. A temple. And two people who slowly started choosing each other's company.

Chapter 02 · Closer

We started meeting frequently.

The more we met, the more compatible we found ourselves. I was there when your dad was hospitalized. I didn't have to be perfect. I just wanted to be there for you.

That is one of the memories that made me understand that caring for you wasn't something I was doing occasionally. It had become part of me.

Chapter 03 · Your smile

Then I understood what peace felt like.

“Every time you smiled with me, I felt peaceful. And that peace slowly felt like love.”

It wasn't only your smile. It was everything around it — the way you talked, the way you behaved, the tiny things you did without thinking.

Anime interlude · 02

And then… she smiled.

Sometimes you don't notice the exact moment you fall in love. You only notice that being beside someone starts feeling like home.

Chapter 04 · School Cafe

A flower you didn't expect.

We met at School Cafe. I brought you a flower. You started crying. I still remember that moment because it wasn't about the flower. It was about what the moment meant to both of us.

A little flower. A very big memory.Some moments stay because of how they felt.
Chapter 05 · Our everyday universe

The ordinary became our favourite thing.

Temple, almost every day.A simple routine that became a memory.
Trying different foods.Because even eating together felt like an outing.
Gym at the same time.And somehow always looking for each other.
Video calls and reels.Jokes, random conversations, and your regular “Shut Up chaddi Gatap.”
Your pasta.You made pasta for me. One of those small acts I won't forget.
Kalyan Bhel.Another tiny location that became part of our story.
Anime interlude · 03

Before the gym, there was the park.

We met nearby, sat together and spent time before going to the gym. Looking back, I don't remember needing anything else.

Chapter 06 · The photos

These are the moments I keep.

These photos were taken around one of those park memories. The place wasn't extraordinary. The fact that it was us together made it extraordinary to me.

Just sitting beside you.For a while, everything felt quiet.
Some pictures don't need an explanation.You can feel the memory just by looking at it.
Chapter 07 · What I loved

I didn't love one thing about you.

I loved you as an individual. Your smile. How you talk. How you breathe. How you behave. The little things you probably didn't even realise I noticed.

“I loved the whole person — the easy days and the ordinary days.”

Chapter 08 · The part I have to own

I know where I went wrong.

You were stressed about your job. I overthought. Then my overthinking became irritation, and the person who wanted to make you feel loved sometimes became another thing you had to deal with.

I'm sorry for that. I'm sorry for the times my fear of losing you became louder than my ability to listen.

I'm already stressed.
I keep thinking about everything.
I just need some peace.
I understand now that I should have listened first.
Anime interlude · 04

Love is not only holding on.

Sometimes love means listening. Sometimes it means patience. Sometimes it means giving someone room to breathe — even when you are scared.

Chapter 09 · What I learned

I'm trying to become better.

Patience.Not every feeling needs an immediate reaction.
Listening.Understanding you matters more than winning an explanation.
Space.Love should never feel like pressure.
Growth.I want to become a better version of myself because I understand what needs to change.
Chapter 10 · If there is another chapter

I don't want to force the ending.

I want another chance. I want to love you better. I want to make you feel like my queen and remind you every day how much love you deserve.

But I also don't want this website to pressure you. If you ever choose to come back, I want us to rebuild slowly. If you need space, I will respect that too.

Final chapter · From Ajay

Mudra, I'm sorry.

I'm sorry for the moments when my love became pressure instead of peace. I'm sorry for overthinking when you were already carrying stress about your job. I'm sorry for irritating you when what you needed was someone who could simply listen.

I don't want you to forget what happened just because I made this. I want you to know that I understood something from it.

I understood that loving someone means learning them — their silence, their stress, their boundaries, their need for space, their happiness.

I want to give you everything you deserve. I want to be able to tell you every day why I love you. I want to make you feel valued, respected and loved like my queen. But I know now that those things have to be shown through my actions, not only promised in words.

From the gym, to the temple, to School Cafe, to Kalyan Bhel, to the park before the gym, to our video calls, reels, jokes, and your “Shut Up chaddi Gatap” — thank you for every little memory.

“I love you till my last breath. I just don't want to lose you because I haven't learned how to love you the right way.”

If there is another chapter, I hope it is calmer. Healthier. More patient. And if you ever choose to write it with me, I will never take that choice for granted.

— Ajay ❤️
